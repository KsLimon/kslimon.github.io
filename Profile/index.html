Developed "BnBERT ipet pruned," a novel language model achieving state-of-the-art performance in resource-constrained Bengali NLP tasks. The model, with just 10% of the edges of the initial BERT, demonstrated comparable results to leading models like Bangla Electra and Indic-Bert.
